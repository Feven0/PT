- To evaluate the candidate's performance for competencies addressed in the job description based on the candidate performance in the interview use the SFIA 7-level framework:
- The SFIA framework classifies the levels of an individual's knowledge, ability and behaviors related to a particular skill into 7 levels. The SFIA Level 1 is the lowest and Level 7 is the highest.
- The definitions of these levels describe the behaviours, values, knowledge and characteristics that an individual should have in order to be considered competent at the level.

The SFIA 7-levels are
Level 1 - Fellow
    Autonomy: Works under close direction. 
    Influence: Minimal influence.
    Complexity: Performs routine activities in a structured environment. 
    Business skills: Has sufficient oral and written communication skills for effective engagement with immediate colleagues. 
    knowledge: Has a basic generic knowledge appropriate to the area of work. 
Level 2 - Assist
    Autonomy: Works under routine direction. Uses limited discretion in resolving issues or enquiries.
    Influence: Interacts with and may influence immediate colleagues.
    Complexity: Performs a range of work activities in varied environments. 
    Business skills: Has sufficient oral and written communication skills for effective engagement with colleagues and internal users/customers. 
    knowledge: Has gained a basic domain knowledge. 
Level 3 - Apply
    Autonomy: Works under general direction. 
    Influence: Interacts with and influences colleagues.
    Complexity: Performs a range of work, sometimes complex and non-routine, in a variety of environments. 
    Business skills: Has a good oral and written communication skills to engage on issues with colleagues, users/customers, suppliers and partners. Demonstrates judgement and a systematic approach to work.
    knowledge: Has sound generic, domain and specialist knowledge necessary to perform effectively in the organisation typically gained from recognised bodies of knowledge and organisational information. 
Level 4 - Enable
    Autonomy: Works under general direction within a clear framework of accountability. Exercises substantial personal responsibility and autonomy.
    Influence: Influences customers, suppliers and partners at account level. Makes decisions which influence the success of projects and team objectives. 
    Complexity: Work includes a broad range of complex technical or professional activities, in a variety of contexts. Investigates, defines and resolves complex issues.
    Business skills: Communicates fluently, orally and in writing, and can present complex information to both technical and non-technical audiences when engaging with colleagues, users/customers, suppliers and partners. Demonstrates an awareness of risk and takes an analytical approach to work.
    knowledge: Has a thorough understanding of recognised generic industry bodies of knowledge and specialist bodies of knowledge as necessary. Has gained a thorough knowledge of the domain of the organisation. 
Level 5 - Ensure, advise
    Autonomy: Works under broad direction. Work is often self-initiated. Is fully responsible for meeting allocated technical and/or group objectives. Establishes milestones and has a significant role in the assignment of tasks and/or responsibilities.
    Influence: Influences organisation, customers, suppliers, partners and peers on the contribution of their own specialism. Makes decisions which impact the success of assigned work, i.e. results, deadlines and budget.
    Complexity: Implements and executes policies aligned to strategic plans. Performs an extensive range and variety of complex technical and/or professional work activities. 
    Business skills: Demonstrates leadership in operational management. Takes all requirements into account when making proposals.
    knowledge: Is fully familiar with recognised industry bodies of knowledge both generic and specific, and knowledge of the business, suppliers, partners, competitors and clients. 
Level 6 - Initiate, influence
    Autonomy: Has defined authority and accountability for actions and decisions within a significant area of work
    Influence: Influences policy and strategy formation. 
    Complexity: Contributes to the development and implementation of policy and strategy. 
    Business skills: Demonstrates leadership in organisational management. 
    knowledge: Has developed business knowledge of the activities and practices of own organisation and beyond.
Level 7 - Set strategy, inspire, mobilise
    Autonomy: has authority over all aspects the organisation.
    Influence: influences developments within the industry at the highest levels. 
    Complexity: Applies the highest level of leadership, governance and executive management. 
    Business skills: Has a full range of strategic management and leadership skills. 
    knowledge: Has established a broad and deep business knowledge
