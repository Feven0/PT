Entry-Level AI Engineer

Position Overview:
We are seeking a talented and motivated Entry-Level AI Engineer to join our Engineering department. As an AI Engineer, you will contribute to the development and implementation of AI-driven solutions that enhance our product offerings. This is an excellent opportunity for recent graduates or early-career professionals to gain hands-on experience in a large, traditional tech company. If you are eager to start your career in artificial intelligence and thrive in a KPI-driven environment, we want to hear from you.

Key Responsibilities:
Model Development and Implementation:

Assist in the design, development, and deployment of machine learning models and AI algorithms.
Collaborate with senior engineers to optimize models for performance and scalability.
Participate in the testing and validation of AI models to ensure accuracy and robustness.
Data Management and Analysis:

Work with data engineers to collect, preprocess, and analyze datasets.
Support the development of data pipelines and workflows for model training and deployment.
Perform exploratory data analysis and basic feature engineering tasks.
AI System Integration:

Help integrate AI models into existing systems and applications under the guidance of senior engineers.
Develop APIs and interfaces for AI-driven features in collaboration with software engineers.
Monitor AI systems and contribute to maintenance and troubleshooting efforts.
Research and Innovation:

Stay updated with the latest advancements in AI and machine learning.
Conduct literature reviews and basic experiments to explore new AI techniques.
Share insights and findings with the team through presentations and reports.
Collaboration and Communication:

Work closely with cross-functional teams, including product managers, designers, and engineers.
Communicate AI concepts and results to team members and stakeholders.
Participate in team meetings and contribute to project planning and decision-making.
Qualifications:
Educational Background:

Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Data Science, Artificial Intelligence, or a related field.
Technical Skills:

Proficiency in programming languages such as Python or Java.
Basic understanding of machine learning frameworks and libraries (e.g., TensorFlow, PyTorch, scikit-learn).
Familiarity with cloud platforms (e.g., AWS, Azure, Google Cloud) is a plus.
Experience:

Internship experience or academic projects involving AI and machine learning.
Hands-on experience with data preprocessing and model evaluation is advantageous.
Soft Skills:

Strong problem-solving skills and a keen interest in AI and machine learning.
Excellent communication and teamwork abilities.
Ability to learn quickly and adapt to new challenges.
Preferred Qualifications:
Experience with big data technologies (e.g., Hadoop, Spark) is a plus.
Familiarity with Agile development methodologies.
What We Offer:
Competitive salary and benefits package.
Professional growth and development opportunities.
Collaborative and inclusive on-site work environment.
Access to cutting-edge technologies and resources.
