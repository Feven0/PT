Senior AI Engineer (Part-time, Remote)

Position Overview:
We are seeking an experienced and innovative Senior AI Engineer to join our Engineering department on a part-time, remote basis. As a Senior AI Engineer, you will play a key role in developing and optimizing advanced AI solutions within our fast-paced, flat-structured team. This position is ideal for professionals who thrive in a dynamic environment and are looking to contribute to a small, agile tech company. If you are a seasoned AI engineer passionate about cutting-edge technologies and remote work flexibility, we want to hear from you.

Key Responsibilities:
Model Development and Implementation:

Lead the design, development, and deployment of sophisticated machine learning models and AI algorithms.
Optimize and fine-tune models to enhance performance, scalability, and robustness.
Ensure the models are interpretable, explainable, and adhere to ethical AI principles.
Data Management and Analysis:

Collaborate with data engineers to oversee data collection, preprocessing, and analysis.
Develop and maintain efficient data pipelines and workflows for continuous integration and deployment.
Conduct advanced exploratory data analysis and feature engineering to improve model accuracy.
AI System Integration:

Oversee the integration of AI models into existing systems and applications.
Work closely with software engineers to develop APIs and interfaces for AI-driven features.
Monitor AI systems, identify performance bottlenecks, and implement solutions.
Research and Innovation:

Stay abreast of the latest advancements in AI, machine learning, and related technologies.
Lead research initiatives and experiments to explore and apply new AI techniques.
Publish findings in peer-reviewed journals and present at industry conferences.
Collaboration and Communication:

Work closely with cross-functional teams, including product managers, designers, and engineers, to align AI solutions with business goals.
Communicate complex AI concepts and results to non-technical stakeholders effectively.
Mentor and guide junior AI engineers and team members.
Qualifications:
Educational Background:

Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Data Science, Artificial Intelligence, or a related field.
Technical Skills:

Proficiency in programming languages such as Python, R, Java, or C++.
Deep understanding of machine learning frameworks and libraries (e.g., TensorFlow, PyTorch, scikit-learn).
Experience with cloud platforms (e.g., AWS, Azure, Google Cloud) and containerization tools (e.g., Docker, Kubernetes).
Experience:

Proven experience in developing and deploying AI models in real-world applications.
Extensive hands-on experience with data preprocessing, feature engineering, and model evaluation.
Knowledge of deep learning, natural language processing, computer vision, and other AI domains.
Soft Skills:

Strong problem-solving skills and ability to think critically and creatively.
Excellent communication and collaboration skills.
Ability to work independently in a remote environment and manage time effectively.
Preferred Qualifications:
Publications or contributions to the AI research community.
Experience with big data technologies (e.g., Hadoop, Spark).
Familiarity with Agile development methodologies.
